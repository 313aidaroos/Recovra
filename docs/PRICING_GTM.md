# Pricing & Go-To-Market

## Core promise
Start with proof of value, not a long software sale.

## Pricing ladder
### Free / low-friction diagnostic
Limited invoice/contract audit with capped volume and no automated submission.

### Recovery Success Fee
Percentage of realized recoveries for eligible categories. Final percentage should vary by vertical, volume and complexity.

### Monitor SaaS
Recurring subscription for continuous monitoring, dashboards, connectors and prevention alerts.

### Enterprise
Annual platform + usage/volume + implementation, SSO/SCIM, custom integrations, data controls, SLAs.

## Land-and-expand
1. One painful category (e.g., parcel/freight or SaaS/AI)
2. Prove recovered dollars
3. Add adjacent modules
4. Connect AP/ERP
5. Pre-payment prevention
6. Negotiation/benchmark intelligence

## Best initial ICPs
- distributors / wholesalers
- importers/exporters
- ecommerce brands
- manufacturers
- multi-location businesses
- tech companies with meaningful AI/cloud/SaaS spend
- companies with 50–2,000 employees and fragmented vendor spend

## Sales artifact
Lead with a Recovery Report:
- spend analyzed
- number/value of findings
- verified recoverable
- annualized preventable waste
- evidence examples
- implementation path

Never market estimated opportunity as realized savings.
