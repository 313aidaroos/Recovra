# Vision

## One sentence
Recovra is the financial recovery intelligence layer between what a business **agreed to pay** and what it **actually paid**.

## North Star
Every payable dollar should be explainable, contract-correct, evidence-backed and recoverable when wrong.

## Platform loop
**Detect -> Explain -> Recover -> Prevent -> Optimize -> Negotiate**

## Why the platform can expand
The same reconciliation primitive appears in freight, cloud, SaaS, telecom, utilities, merchant processing, procurement and vendor billing. Each vertical contributes reusable ingestion, normalization, anomaly and recovery knowledge.

## Long-term moats
- Proprietary charge/rate/contract normalization graph
- Cross-vendor anomaly patterns
- Outcome data: what disputes vendors accept and why
- Benchmark intelligence (privacy-preserving and contract-compliant)
- Embedded workflows inside ERP/AP/TMS/procurement systems
- Recovery ledger proving actual ROI
- Deep connector surface

## Strategic buyers/partners (not promises)
ERP, AP automation, procurement, corporate card/spend management, logistics/TMS, FinOps/cloud management and banking platforms could all benefit from embedding recovery/prevention capabilities.
